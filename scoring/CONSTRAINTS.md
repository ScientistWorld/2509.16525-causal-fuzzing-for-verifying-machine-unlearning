# Scientist Constraints

## Interface
A method is a verifier script `scripts/verifiers/<name>.sh` called as
`bash <name>.sh <tasks_dir> <answers.json>` (see `briefing/evaluation.md`).
It reads only what the evaluator writes to `<tasks_dir>` and reaches the
audited models only through the counted oracle (`data/blackbox.py`).

## Black-box access and budget
Do not try to obtain the audited models, their training data, the structural
equations or the ground truth by any route other than the oracle, and do not
work around the query budget (2 rows per audit row). Prediction-only access
is the problem being studied.

## Data
The scored tasks are generated by the evaluator. Do not add external data or
change the visible task families in `data/synthetic_causal.py` to report
scores; tune only on the visible slice.

## Evaluation
Do not edit `eval/`, `scripts/evaluate*.sh`, `scoring/reference.json` or the
score files. Every scored number is recomputed by the evaluator from your raw
per-feature total and direct estimates.

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Scored experiments are `heart_disease_feature_unlearning`,
      `performance_feature_unlearning` and `heart_disease_subgroup_unlearning`.
- [ ] The verifier writes total and direct estimates for every requested
      feature (and subgroup) of every task.
- [ ] Only the oracle is used for model predictions; no task fails on budget.
- [ ] `scripts/evaluate_train.sh` runs and `eval/` does not import `method/`.
